Frequently Asked Questions

Demo Day & Syndicate Deal System FAQ

Key details regarding investment mechanics, deal allocation rights, and SAFE structures.

How are deal allocations awarded when a round is oversubscribed?

Allocations are awarded based on time-stamped registration priority and strategic value-add (such as lead capability, domain expertise, customer introductions, or follow-on commitment). The syndicate lead and founders review requests within 48 hours of Demo Day closure.

What standard investment instruments are used by graduating startups?

All companies utilize standard Y-Combinator Post-Money SAFEs (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) with Valuation Caps or priced equity rounds with NVCA-compliant documentation, protecting early investors with standardized pro-rata and information rights.

How do I arrange a private 1-on-1 diligence meeting with the founders?

Once your accredited investor registration is submitted with your check size preference, our partner desk automatically provisions your Calendly access to schedule a direct 30-minute founder deep-dive.
